Data Privacy & Protection

BookHive Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to our literary mission. We are committed to protecting your personal data, maintaining reader confidentiality, and safeguarding author manuscripts.

Quick Privacy Summary: BookHive does not sell your personal data. We only collect the minimal information needed to operate reader profiles, track personal reading progress, and process author book submissions securely.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when interacting with BookHive:

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, password hashes, and user role (Reader, Author, or Administrator).
  • Reading Activity: Books added to your reading shelf, reading progress percentages, bookmark positions, and user reviews.
  • Author Content: Uploaded manuscript PDFs, cover artwork, author biographies, book descriptions, and genre classifications.
  • Technical Metadata: Browser type, approximate location, device viewport, and security access logs to protect platform integrity.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is exclusively utilized for genuine platform operations:

  • To provide seamless digital book reading experiences through our embedded manuscript reader.
  • To allow authors to review reader engagement, community feedback, and book distribution statistics.
  • To send essential account notifications, email verifications, and security password resets.
  • To prevent fraudulent uploads, unauthorized access, and malicious activity.

3. Author Manuscript & File Security

Author manuscripts uploaded in PDF format are stored in secure object storage with cryptographic integrity checks. We do not index private draft manuscripts into public search engines without the author's explicit publication consent.

4. Cookies & Session Storage

We utilize strictly necessary session cookies and local storage to retain your authentication tokens, reader view mode preferences (e.g. font size, light/dark reading mode), and active navigation state. We do not deploy third-party advertising tracking cookies.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

You maintain complete control over your personal data under global data protection principles:

  • Access & Correction: You can inspect and update your profile details at any time from your Account Settings.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request complete deletion of your account and associated reading data by reaching out to privacy@bookhive.org.
  • Author Manuscript Deletion: Authors can unpublish or remove their manuscripts from public distribution directly from the Author Studio.

Copyright & Ownership

Intellectual Property Policy

BookHive is founded on deep respect for human creativity. Authors retain full ownership of their manuscripts, while readers enjoy licensed personal reading access.

Key Copyright Guarantee: Authors retain 100% of their copyright and intellectual property. You do NOT surrender ownership when uploading your book to BookHive.

1. Author Copyright Retention

When you upload a manuscript, cover design, or excerpt to BookHive:

  • You remain the sole owner of your literary work, copyright, and moral rights.
  • You grant BookHive a limited, non-exclusive, revocable worldwide license solely to store, convert, and render your manuscript for digital reading and preview by registered readers.
  • You are completely free to publish, sell, or distribute your book across other platforms, publishers, or physical mediums without restriction.

2. Reader Fair Use & Non-Commercial License

Readers accessing books through the BookHive digital reader receive a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to read the publication for educational and recreational purposes. Readers may not:

  • Redistribute, resell, or publicly broadcast the author's raw manuscript files.
  • Scrape, reverse-engineer, or systematically harvest manuscripts for commercial training or digital piracy.

3. Copyright Infringement & DMCA Takedown Procedure

BookHive respects the intellectual property rights of all content creators. If you believe your copyrighted work has been reproduced on BookHive without authorization, please submit a formal takedown notice containing:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  2. The specific BookHive book URL or author title of the allegedly infringing material.
  3. Your contact information (name, address, telephone number, and email).
  4. A statement of good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, agent, or law.
  5. Your physical or electronic signature.

Please send all infringement notifications directly to our designated copyright agent at copyright@bookhive.org.

4. BookHive Brand & Platform Assets

The BookHive name, honeycomb book logo, graphic design tokens, software code, and user interface elements are protected trademarks and intellectual property of BookHive.
